Myth vs fact

Told you need contrast?

Myth 1 of 3
MYTH

Contrast stains your organs

FACT

It's a temporary helper.

It helps tissues show up clearly, then your body clears it, mostly in urine.

Source: RadiologyInfo.org (ACR, RSNA)
Myth 2 of 3
MYTH

A shellfish allergy rules it out

FACT

It doesn't raise your risk.

Still tell us about every allergy before your scan.

Source: AAAAI
Myth 3 of 3
MYTH

Every scan needs contrast

FACT

Many don't.

Most brain, spine and knee MRIs are done without it.

Source: advancedimg.com
