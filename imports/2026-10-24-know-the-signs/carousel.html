Get these changes checked

A new lump in the breast or underarm.

Swelling or thickening, even without a lump.

Skin dimpling, redness or flaky skin.

A nipple that turns inward, or discharge that isn't milk.

Also: a change in size or shape, or new pain in one area. Sources: CDC, Symptoms of Breast Cancer; American Cancer Society.

Good to know

A change isn't a diagnosis.

Mostbreast lumps are not cancer. Get every new one checked.
Normalchanges happen with periods, pregnancy and menopause.
Underarmlumps and swollen glands count, too.
SilentSome breast cancers cause no signs at all.

Sources: American Cancer Society; CDC.
