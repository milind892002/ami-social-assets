Advanced Medical Imaging
If your scan uses contrast

Feeling warm is normal.

A small IV goes in your arm: just a quick pinch.

A warm flush or metal taste lasts only seconds.

An urge to pee is common, and it passes.

Afterward, drink extra water.

Many CT scans need no contrast at all. Sources: NIH MedlinePlus; RadiologyInfo.org (ACR, RSNA).

Before your CT scan

Contrast scan? You may skip food for a few hours. We'll tell you.

Had a reaction to contrast or iodine before? Tell us.

Tell us about kidney problems, diabetes or metformin.

Pregnant, or might be? Tell us first.

Leave jewelry at home. Source: NIH MedlinePlus.
