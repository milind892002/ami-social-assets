Before your 3T MRI

Tell us about any implant, pacemaker, stent or metal in your body. Some cleared for 1.5T aren't cleared for 3T.

Leave jewelry, watches and hair clips at home.

Wear comfy clothes with no metal zippers or underwire.

Arrive 15 minutes early for forms and safety screening.
