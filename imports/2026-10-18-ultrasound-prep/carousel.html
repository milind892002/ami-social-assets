Prep by type

Know your scan, know your prep.

AbdomenNo food for 8 to 12 hours before. Water is OK.
PelvisDrink 32 oz of water 1 hour before. Don't empty your bladder.
Thyroid, breastNo special prep needed.
VascularNo special prep needed.

Always follow the instructions from your doctor or our team. Sources: RadiologyInfo.org (ACR, RSNA); NIH MedlinePlus.
