Myth vs fact

3 myths, busted.

1
Myth: every scan uses radiationFact: MRI uses a magnet and radio waves. Ultrasound uses sound waves. No radiation.
2
Myth: X-ray doses are bigFact: a chest X-ray is about the same as 10 days of the natural radiation all around us.
3
Myth: it's rude to askFact: please ask. Your doctor weighs the benefit of every scan against its small risk.

Sources: CDC (2026); RadiologyInfo.org, from the ACR and RSNA.
