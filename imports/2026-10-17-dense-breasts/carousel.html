What "dense" means

Normal tissue, just harder to read.

NormalMore gland and fiber tissue than fat. Not a disease.
UnseenYou can't feel it. Only a mammogram shows it.
Harderto read: dense tissue and tumors both look white.
RiskDensity modestly raises risk. It's one factor of several.

Sources: American Cancer Society; CDC; FDA.

Options to ask about

Dense? Your doctor may suggest:

A 3D mammogram, which looks at tissue layer by layer.

A breast ultrasound, with no radiation.

A breast MRI, mainly for higher-risk women.
