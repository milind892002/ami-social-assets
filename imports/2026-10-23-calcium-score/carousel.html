Is it right for you?

Often used for adults 40 to 75 with:

High cholesterol or high blood pressure.

Diabetes, or a smoking history.

A family history of heart attack or stroke.

Unsure about starting a statin.

Heart groups (ACC/AHA) support it for some adults; the USPSTF says evidence is not enough to screen everyone. Ask your doctor. Chest pain now? Call 911.

What your score means

0: no calcium plaque detected.

1 to 99: mild plaque.

100 to 399: moderate plaque.

400 or more: extensive plaque.

Your doctor reads your score with your other risk factors. Sources: NIH NHLBI; AMI report score ranges.
