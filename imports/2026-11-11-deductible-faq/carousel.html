What a deductible means for a scan.

It's what you pay for covered care before your plan starts to pay.

Not met yet? You may pay your plan's full rate for the scan yourself.

Some care, like certain preventive screenings, is covered before it.

Source: HealthCare.gov glossary, “Deductible.” Every plan is different, so check your plan details.
