Advanced Medical Imaging
What it is

A quick CT scan that checks your heart arteries for calcium plaque.

A radiologist turns the pictures into one number: your calcium score. It helps you and your doctor judge your risk of a future heart attack. A score of 0 means no calcium plaque was found.

Sources: 2018 ACC/AHA cholesterol guideline (ACC.org); U.S. Preventive Services Task Force, 2018.

Advanced Medical Imaging
Is it for you?

Often used for adults 40 to 75 with:

High cholesterol or high blood pressure.

Diabetes, or a smoking history.

A family history of heart attack or stroke.

A statin decision on the table.

ACC/AHA guidelines support it when the statin decision is uncertain; the USPSTF says the evidence isn't enough to screen everyone. Ask your doctor. Not for people with a stent, bypass or known heart disease. Chest pain now? Call 911.
