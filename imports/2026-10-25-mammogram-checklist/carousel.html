The morning of

Skip, wear, arrive.

Skip deodorant, powder, lotion and perfume on your underarms and breasts.

Wear a top with pants or a skirt, so only your top comes off.

Leave necklaces at home.

Arrive 15 minutes early.

Deodorant and powders can show up as white spots on the images. Sources: American Cancer Society; FDA.
